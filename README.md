# Streaming Platform Strategy Analysis

## Project Overview 
This project analyzes the content strategy of major streaming platforms and their competitive positions within the streaming industry using publicly available datasets.

## Objective
Identify strategic opportunities for Hulu to strengthen its market position by evaluating its content quality and library size against competitors, applying Porter's Five Forces and a SWOT analysis to surface a data-driven recommendation for improving subscriber retention.

## Dataseet
Movies and TV Shows on Streaming Platforms dataset.

## Methodology
- Data Cleaning 
- Exploratory Data Analysis 
- Platform Comparison 
- Strategic Recommendation

## Key Findings 
- Hulu achieves the highest average RottenTomatoes scores among the platforms compared, for both movies and TV shows
- Hulu's content library is smaller than Netflix's and Prime Video's, particularly for movies
- Buyer power and competitive rivalry in streaming are very high, giving consumers strong leverage over platform choice
- Hulu's key strengths include a large subscriber base and bundled offerings with Disney+ and ESPN, while its main weakness is a smaller,       primarily U.S.-only content library. 
- Expanding content variety could reduce churn from 4.9% to 2%, retaining an estimated ~1.86M additional subscribers annually
